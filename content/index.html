Welcome to Apache Traffic Server!¶

Apache Traffic Server™ is a powerful, versatile HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 compliant caching proxy server. Originally a commercial product, it was generously donated to the Apache Foundation by Yahoo! and is now utilized by numerous major CDNs and content owners.

Explore our features, get support, and join the community to leverage the full potential of Apache Traffic Server.

Key Features¶

Caching¶

Enhance response time, reduce server load, and minimize bandwidth consumption by caching frequently-requested web pages, images, and web service calls.

Proxying¶

Effortlessly implement keep-alive, content filtering, anonymization, or load balancing through a flexible proxy layer.

Fast¶

Designed to scale efficiently on modern SMP hardware, capable of handling tens of thousands of requests per second.

Extensible¶

Utilize APIs to develop custom plugins for tasks ranging from HTTP header modification to implementing bespoke cache algorithms.

Proven¶

Battle-tested at Yahoo!, processing over 400TB of data daily as both forward and reverse proxies. Explore our Customers page for insights into our corporate users and supporters.
